FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does pricing work for a billing company?

Per bill submitted, flat — no per-seat, per-client, per-provider, or per-NPI fees. Adding a client or a biller never costs you anything. Book a demo and we'll quote your volume.

Can we put MindBill inside our own client portal?

Yes — embeddable React and Angular components render the billing experience in your portal, backed by the same API. See the developer platform for docs and a sandbox.

Can you migrate us off daisyBill or another system?

Yes — on-site and hands-on: we migrate you in a single day, then stay on-site the first week, with a parallel run so nothing slips during the switch.

Do you support both med-legal and treatment clients?

Yes. Med-legal (QME / AME / IME on the MLFS) and treatment-side workers' comp billing, plus RFAs — every client on one platform.

Is it HIPAA-compliant?

Yes — MindBill is HIPAA-compliant and a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is included.
