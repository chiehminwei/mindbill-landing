FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does pricing work for a large group?

Per bill submitted — flat, regardless of how many evaluators, users, locations, or NPIs you add. Book a demo and we'll quote your volume.

Can you migrate us off daisyBill or another system?

Yes — on-site and hands-on: we migrate you in a single day, then stay on-site the first week, with a parallel run so nothing slips during the switch.

Can MindBill connect to our existing systems?

Yes — a modern API, webhooks, and embeddable components let you connect scheduling, EHR, or your own portals. See the developer platform for docs and a sandbox.

Is it HIPAA-compliant?

Yes — MindBill is HIPAA-compliant and a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is included.
