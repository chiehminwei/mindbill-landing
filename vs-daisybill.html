FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can you migrate us off daisyBill or another system?

Yes — on-site and hands-on: we migrate you in a single day, then stay on-site the first week, with a parallel run so nothing slips during the switch.

Will my bills get paid as fast as they do on daisyBill?

Yes — electronic bills travel the same clearinghouse rails either way, and under California DWC rules payers must act on an e-bill within 15 working days. In practice many are paid in about 7 days. What changes is how much of your team's day the billing eats.

What does it cost?

You pay per bill submitted — that's it. No subscriptions, no per-seat fees, no per-provider or per-NPI fees. Book a demo and we'll quote your volume.

Do you support both med-legal and treatment billing?

Yes. Med-legal (QME / AME / IME on the MLFS) and treatment-side workers' comp billing, plus RFAs — one platform, same rails.

Is it HIPAA-compliant?

Yes — MindBill is HIPAA-compliant and a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is included.
