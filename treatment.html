How it works

Authorization to final dollar.

The treatment revenue cycle, end to end — on the same rails our med-legal customers collect on.

1 · RFA & authorization

Request authorization for treatment and keep every RFA tracked against its claim — nothing waits in a fax tray.

2 · Bill on the fee schedule

Procedure and ICD-10 templates, payer auto-detected from the claim number, and blocking checks that catch bad bills before they go out.

3 · E-billed & tracked

A clean 837 goes to the payer's clearinghouse; 999/277 acknowledgments and electronic EORs (835) post back automatically.

4 · Underpaid? Appealed.

Every EOR is compared to the fee schedule. Underpayments trigger a pre-filled second review — automatically.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you support treatment billing, or just med-legal?

Both. Med-legal (QME / AME / IME on the MLFS) and treatment-side workers' comp billing — office visits through surgery on the official fee schedule, plus RFAs — run on the same rails: AI bill entry, e-billing, and automatic second reviews.

Do you handle RFAs?

Yes — request authorization and track every RFA against its claim inside MindBill.

Which clearinghouses and payers do you support?

The California workers'-comp clearinghouses we integrate with directly — Carisk, Jopari, and Data Dimensions — plus fax and mail fallback for non-electronic payers. New integrations typically ship in about two weeks.

Can you migrate us off daisyBill or another system?

Yes — on-site and hands-on: we migrate you in a single day, then stay on-site the first week, with a parallel run so nothing slips during the switch.

Is it HIPAA-compliant?

Yes — MindBill is HIPAA-compliant and a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is included.
