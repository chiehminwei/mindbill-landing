The whole bill lifecycle

From bill entry to final dollar collected.

Purpose-built for California workers' comp — med-legal and treatment — not a generic biller with work-comp bolted on.

1

Bill entry with a built-in scrub

Med-legal and treatment bills on the official fee schedules — MLFS codes & modifier stacking (ML200–ML205, 92–98), pre-built procedure & ICD-10 templates, carrier auto-detected from the claim number, CMS-1500 / DWC SBR-1 ready. Blocking checks catch bad bills before they go out.

2

Underpayments caught & appealed

Every EOR is compared to the fee schedule. Underpayments are flagged, Second Reviews (SBR-1) are pre-filled with appeal language that works, and IBR + audit complaints are one click away.

3

Clearinghouse submission, handled

Clean electronic bills to Carisk, Jopari, and Data Dimensions — with fax and mail fallback for non-electronic payers. Inbound payer-fax auto-matching included.

4

Same-day corrections

Catch a mistake before it reaches the clearinghouse? Edit and resubmit the same day — no waiting period, with full bill history and team notes.

5

AI report parsing & autofill

Drop in the med-legal report and AI extracts the patient, ICD-10 diagnoses, claim & ADJ numbers, and rendering provider — then picks the right payer. A clean bill, ready to review, in about 10 seconds.

6

No caps on users or providers

Add every biller and every rendering provider / NPI you want. We never charge per seat or per NPI — the only thing you pay for is a bill submitted.

Electronic billing, end to end

Get paid in days, not weeks.

MindBill integrates directly with California's workers'-comp clearinghouses and uses a proprietary routing algorithm to reach each payer in the fewest hops — so bills land fast and get paid fast, tracked all the way to payment.

1 · Submit

A clean 837P is generated from your bill and routed to the right clearinghouse — Carisk, Jopari, or Data Dimensions.

2 · Confirm

Real-time 999 / 277 acknowledgments confirm the payer received and accepted the bill — no more wondering if it landed.

3 · Reconcile

Electronic EORs (835) post back automatically and are checked against the fee schedule — underpayments flagged on the spot.

4 · Recover

Underpaid? A Second Review is pre-filled and queued; non-electronic payers fall back to fax & mail. Nothing slips.

Electronic billing helps reduce delays compared with paper. Under California DWC e-billing rules, payers must act on an electronic bill within 15 working days.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which clearinghouses and payers do you support?

The California workers'-comp clearinghouses we integrate with directly — Carisk, Jopari, and Data Dimensions — plus fax and mail fallback for non-electronic payers. New integrations typically ship in about two weeks.

Do you handle treatment billing, or just med-legal?

Both. Med-legal (QME / AME / IME on the MLFS) and treatment-side workers' comp billing — office visits through surgery on the official fee schedule, plus RFAs — run on the same rails: AI bill entry, e-billing, and automatic second reviews.

How does electronic billing get me paid faster?

Electronic bills are received and adjudicated far faster than paper. Under California DWC rules, payers must act on an e-bill within 15 working days — and in practice many are paid in about 7 days. You get 999/277 acknowledgments and electronic EORs (835) back automatically.

Can you migrate us off daisyBill or another system?

Yes — on-site and hands-on: we migrate you in a single day, then stay on-site the first week, with a parallel run so nothing slips during the switch.

Do you handle second reviews, IBR, and audit complaints?

Yes. Underpayments are flagged automatically against the fee schedule, Second Reviews are pre-filled (SBR-1 + CMS-1500 attached), and we escalate to IBR and audit complaints when needed.

Is it HIPAA-compliant?

Yes — MindBill is HIPAA-compliant and a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is included.
